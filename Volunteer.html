Become a Volunteer

Volunteers are an important part of Second Chance Paw & Hope Animal Rescue. By giving your time and skills, you can help us care for our furr babies and support our rescue activities.

Whether you enjoy working directly with animals, helping at events or supporting our organisation, there is a way for you to make a difference.

Why Volunteer?

Animal Care

Help with feeding, exercising and caring for rescued cats and dogs.

Events and Fundraising

Help organise and support fundraising events that benefit rescued animals.

Community Support

Help spread awareness about animal rescue, adoption and responsible pet ownership.

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Animal Care: Assist with the daily care and wellbeing of rescued animals.
  • Foster Support: Support animals that are staying in temporary foster homes.
  • Events: Help with adoption days, fundraising events and community activities.
  • Administration: Assist with basic administrative and organisational tasks.
